How to Score

👆
Tap to Fulfill

Tap a station to fulfill the matching character's need before their timer runs out. Miss it and you lose a life.

🔥
Combo Streak

Fulfill needs back-to-back to build streaks and multiply your score.

1x 2x 3x Max multiplier

📈
Difficulty Scaling

Every 1,000 points the pressure rises — characters spawn faster and timers get shorter. The stress meter fills when you miss or lose lives.
