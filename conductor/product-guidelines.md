# Product Guidelines: Mamad Manager (Safe Room Chaos)

## Brand Tone & Messaging
The game's communication should be:
- **Humorous and Lighthearted:** Maintain a cozy, fun vibe even during intense gameplay sessions.
- **Relatable and Warm:** Use language that resonates with the shared experience of being in a safe room, keeping it humorous without being insensitive.

## Visual Identity & Art Style
The game's visual style will be:
- **Vibrant Cartoonish with Hand-Drawn Elements:** High-contrast characters and objects with expressive facial expressions and thick outlines, combined with sketch-like textures to create a personal, relatable feel.
- **Chaos & Contrast:** Use bright, bold colors for interactive elements (action stations, timers) and more muted, cozy tones for the "Mamad" background.
- **Culturally Specific Assets:** Incorporate iconic Israeli items into the art, such as:
    - The heavy orange/white "Mamad" door and massive handle.
    - The round air filter machine (Baraf).
    - Folding mattresses and pillows.
    - Recognizable snack packaging (Bamba/Bisli).

## User Experience (UX) & UI Design
The interface will focus on:
- **Clarity and Speed:** High-contrast icons and visual timers (Green -> Yellow -> Red) for immediate task identification and urgency.
- **Minimalist Overlay:** A clean UI that doesn't distract from the character interactions, featuring a prominent "Overall Stress Meter" thermometer at the top.
- **Tactile Feedback:** Providing clear visual and haptic feedback for every successful or failed gesture to reinforce the physical rhythm of the game.
