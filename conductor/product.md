# Product Definition: Mamad Manager (Safe Room Chaos)

## Target Audience
The game is designed for:
- **Casual Mobile Gamers:** Players looking for fast-paced, engaging, and quick play sessions on the go.
- **Relatable Israeli Context:** People familiar with the "Mamad" (safe room) experience who can appreciate the humorous and cozy take on this shared reality.

## Player Goals & Experience
The primary objectives for the player are:
- **Gesture Mastery:** Developing reflexes and physical rhythm to handle the diverse touch interactions required by the five action stations (Hold, Drag & Drop, Continuous Swipe, Quick Swipes, and Rapid Tapping).
- **Stress Management:** Keeping the overall "Stress Meter" low by fulfilling the needs of adults, kids, and the dog before their individual timers expire.
- **Pass the Time:** Providing a fun, distracting, and meaningful way to pass the time while staying in a shelter.
- **Life-Based Progression:** Manage 3 "Rocket" lives—losing one for every missed character—and strive for high scores before running out.
- **Dynamic Challenge:** Master the increasing pace as spawn rates and character timers intensify based on your performance.
- **Endless Survival:** Competing for a spot on the local leaderboard by surviving as long as possible as the pace intensifies.

# Initial Concept

## Game Title: Mamad Manager (Safe Room Chaos)
**The Premise:** You are the designated "manager" of a crowded Israeli safe room (Mamad). The siren has sounded, the heavy iron door is shut, and now you have to keep everyone inside calm until the Home Front Command gives the all-clear. You must juggle the needs of stressed adults, bored kids, and an energetic dog. If the room's overall "Stress Meter" maxes out, it's Game Over!

### 1. Core Mechanics & Gameplay
The game is a fast-paced time-management and reflex game.
Characters appear at the top half of the screen with a "thought bubble" showing what they need and a visual timer (a circle that shrinks and changes color from Green to Yellow to Blinking Red).
You operate five interactive stations at the bottom of the screen to fulfill their needs before their timers run out.

*   **The Adults:** They usually ask for Water, Phone Charging, or Cellular Reception.
*   **The Kids:** They ask for Snacks (Bamba or Bisli) or Entertainment.
*   **The Dog:** Occasionally pops up and barks. Barking speeds up the stress timers of everyone else in the room. You must quickly pet or distract the dog to restore quiet.

### 2. The Five Action Stations
Each station requires a different touch gesture, forcing the player to constantly switch their physical rhythm:
*   **Water Pitcher (Action: Touch & Hold):** Press and hold the screen to pour water into a cup. Release exactly when full. Early release = rejection. Overfill = 3s lock for "cleanup."
*   **Charging Station (Action: Drag & Drop):** Drag a dangling plug into the port of a moving smartphone.
*   **The Reception Hunter (Action: Continuous Swipe):** Swipe a hand left/right to find the "sweet spot" (3 bars) and hold for 2s to send a WhatsApp.
*   **Snack Sorter (Action: Quick Swipes):** Swipe right for Bamba, left for Bisli. Wrong snack causes an instant timer drop.
*   **Dog Distraction (Action: Rapid Tapping):** Tap a bouncing tennis ball 3-4 times to throw it to the dog.

### 3. Visuals & Art Style
*   **The Vibe:** 2D, colorful, cartoonish, and slightly chaotic, but with a cozy, humorous undertone.
*   **The Background:** A classic Israeli Mamad with the iconic heavy orange/white door, air filter (Baraf), folding mattress, and a guitar.
*   **The Characters:** Expressive and relatable (Saba in a tank top, teenager, parent in pajamas, hyperactive Canaanite mix dog).
*   **UI:** Clean and minimal. Big thermometer-style "Overall Stress Meter" at the top.

### 4. Scoring & Progression System
*   **Points:** Base points for delivery, double points for "Green" speed bonus.
*   **Combos:** Visual multipliers (x2, x3) for consecutive mistake-free fulfillments.
*   **Lives:** 3 Rockets. A rocket is lost when a character timer expires.
*   **End Game:** Endless survival mode. Progressively faster. Ends when lives reach 0 or 100% stress.
*   **Leaderboard:** Local Top 10 with score-based ranks:
    - 0-500: Mamad Rookie
    - 501-1500: Snack Commander
    - 1501-3000: Safe Room Pro
    - 3001+: Chief of Home Front

### 5. Settings & Accessibility
*   **Master Mute:** Giant button for instant silence.
*   **Music/SFX Toggles:** Tense/funny Klezmer/Middle Eastern electronic beat.
*   **Vibration Toggle:** Haptic feedback for urgency when muted.
